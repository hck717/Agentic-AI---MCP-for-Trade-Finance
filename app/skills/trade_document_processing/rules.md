# Compliance Rules Reference

## UCP 600 (Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits)

### Article 14: Standard for Examination of Documents
- **(d):** Data in a document, when read in context with the credit, the document itself and international standard banking practice, need not be identical to, but must not conflict with, data in that document, any other stipulated document or the credit.
- **(e):** In documents other than the commercial invoice, the description of the goods, services or performance, if stated, may be in general terms not conflicting with their description in the credit.

### Article 18: Commercial Invoice
- **(c):** The description of the goods, services or performance in a commercial invoice must correspond with that in the credit.

---

## ISBP 745 (International Standard Banking Practice)

### Article E26: Bill of Lading - Goods Description
- A goods description on a bill of lading may be in general terms not inconsistent with the description in the credit.
- **Example:** LC calls for "iPhone 15". B/L may state "Electronic Goods" or "Mobile Phones".

### Article C6: Ports
- Ports may be stated with or without the country name.
- **Example:** "Shanghai" is acceptable for "Chinese Port".
